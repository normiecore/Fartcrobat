## Fixes from the first weeks of use

- **Bookmarks keep your zoom.** Clicking a bookmark goes to its page at the zoom you are reading at. In 0.4.1, bookmarks in combined briefs switched to Fit Page, which could shrink the pages to 8%. A bookmark saved at a set zoom (say 150%) still opens at that zoom.
- **Fit Width and Fit Page ignore oversized pages.** A phone photo merged into a brief at full size no longer makes every other page tiny. The photo page is simply larger than the window and scrolls.
- **Going to a page shows it** even when a much wider page has scrolled the view sideways.
- **Signatures copied from other documents are explained.** When signed documents (such as DocuSign PDFs) are combined into one bundle, their signatures can no longer be verified in the bundle. Inkmoor still marks them invalid, as other PDF tools do, but now says they were copied from other documents and that the original signed document is the one to check. The signer's name is shown again, and these signatures no longer get a "Rev." number.
