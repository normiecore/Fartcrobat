## Fixes from the first weeks of use

- **Bookmarks keep your zoom.** Clicking a bookmark goes to its page at the zoom you are reading at. In 0.4.1, bookmarks in combined briefs switched to Fit Page, which could shrink the pages to 8%. A bookmark saved at a set zoom (say 150%) still opens at that zoom.
- **Fit Width and Fit Page ignore oversized pages.** A phone photo merged into a brief at full size no longer makes every other page tiny. The photo page is simply larger than the window and scrolls.
- **The page you are reading stays in view** when a much wider page makes the document scroll sideways: on opening, when going to a page, and when scrolling past the wide page.
- **Signatures whose signed content is missing are explained.** When signed documents (such as DocuSign PDFs) are combined into one bundle, their signatures can no longer be verified in the bundle. Inkmoor still marks them invalid, as other PDF tools do, and now says why: what was signed is not in this document, usually because the signature was copied from another document, so the original signed document is the one to check. The signer's name is shown again, and these signatures no longer get a "Rev." number or count as certifying the bundle.

**Still on Fartcrobat or Inkmoor 0.4.0?** Those copies look for updates at the old address and won't find this release. Install 0.4.2 by hand once (see [0.4.1](https://github.com/normiecore/Inkmoor/releases/tag/v0.4.1) for how); from 0.4.1 on, Inkmoor updates itself.
