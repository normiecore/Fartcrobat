## What’s new

- **Watch the AI’s edits arrive:** when Claude or ChatGPT proposes changes, they appear on the page one after another, in reading order. The old wording is struck through and the new text writes in over it. Font and size changes are labelled, and guides show where margins moved. Show all skips ahead.
- **Accept or reject each change:** the Review edits panel lists every change as its own card with Accept edit and Reject edit. Hover a card and the change is circled on the page; click it to go there. Up and Down move between cards, A accepts and R rejects. You can change your mind while other changes are still waiting, and the accepted ones are applied together as one Undo step.
- **Rewrites and new designs, piece by piece:** a rewrite that flows text through the page’s columns gets a card per paragraph (rejecting one keeps your original wording). A new design gets a card per section (rejecting one leaves that section out).
- **Sharp at any zoom:** proposed changes are drawn at full resolution, like the document itself, instead of a picture that blurred when you zoomed in.
- **Move the AI companion anywhere:** drag it by any part that isn’t text; a click still opens the chat. It moves out of the way of the review panel while you review.
- **Snappier:** reviewing changes no longer copies the whole document for every decision, and the app does less work while pages render.

## Fixed

- Undoing a text edit and then making the same edit again failed with an error. Undoing an added or replaced picture and then adding the same picture again did nothing.
- In the review, text selection and links on the page keep working, and only the change markers take clicks.

## After updating

Add the Claude Desktop extension again from **Preferences → AI apps → Add to Claude Desktop**, then restart Claude Desktop. The extension keeps its own copy of Fartcrobat’s helper, so Claude only learns that each change can now be accepted or rejected on its own once it is replaced. Restart ChatGPT Desktop if you use its local MCP connection; its helper updates by itself.

Coming from 0.2.0 or 0.2.1? 0.3.0 includes everything in [0.2.1](https://github.com/normiecore/Fartcrobat/releases/tag/v0.2.1) and [0.2.2](https://github.com/normiecore/Fartcrobat/releases/tag/v0.2.2), including companion chat without an invitation and one download per computer.
