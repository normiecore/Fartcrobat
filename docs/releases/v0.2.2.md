## What’s fixed

- **The companion chat waits for you after AI apps disconnect:** if you clicked Chat here and every AI app then disconnected before a conversation joined, the companion disappeared but its offer stayed open, and the next AI app to use Fartcrobat, even hours later, joined the chat without you opening it again. Now the chat closes when the companion disappears, and a conversation joins only after you click Chat here again.

Windows installers remain unsigned; macOS builds are ad-hoc signed.
