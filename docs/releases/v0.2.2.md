## What’s fixed

- **The companion chat stays where you can close it:** in 0.2.1, if you clicked Chat here and your AI app then quit before a conversation connected, the companion disappeared while its chat stayed open, and the next conversation to use Fartcrobat, even hours later, connected to it. The companion now stays on screen once you open the chat, and closing the chat there keeps every conversation out until you open it again.

## Coming from 0.2.0

0.2.2 includes everything in [0.2.1](https://github.com/normiecore/Fartcrobat/releases/tag/v0.2.1): companion chat without an invitation, on-document edit review, one download per computer, Connect ChatGPT Desktop in installed copies, and no request to Google on Linux.

Add the Claude Desktop extension again from **Preferences → AI apps → Add to Claude Desktop**, then restart Claude Desktop. The extension keeps its own copy of Fartcrobat's helper, and the one added from 0.2.0 does not connect to the companion chat by itself: Fartcrobat says so in the chat and in Preferences → AI apps until it is replaced. Restart ChatGPT Desktop if you use its local MCP connection.

Windows installers remain unsigned; macOS builds are ad-hoc signed.
